What does "hedged" mean?

The fund buys shares and, at the same time, sells matching futures, so a market fall hurts the share and helps the future. It is protection against falls, but it also caps the gain. The tracker's "unhedged derivative positions" card shows bets that are not matched this way. RedHex had none in September or October.

What are REITs and InvITs?

REITs own office buildings and collect rent. InvITs own things like highways and collect tolls. You can buy small units of them on the stock exchange, so a fund can hold them.

What is a securitised loan?

A bank or lender bundles many small loans (for example vehicle loans) and sells the bundle to investors. The fund buys a slice. The slice is rated, and the rating usually shows "(SO)", meaning structured obligation.

What do AAA, AA and A mean?

They are credit ratings from agencies like CRISIL, ICRA, CARE and India Ratings. AAA is the safest. Each step down means a higher chance that the borrower struggles to pay. "AA family" here includes AA+, AA and AA−; "A family" includes A+ and A. Sovereign means the government.

How to read these numbers

Volatility is how much the NAV wobbles. Max drawdown is the biggest fall from a high point. Sharpe compares return with the wobble after the 6.5% a safe deposit might give. Beta of 0.05 means the fund has hardly moved with the Nifty 50 so far. With only 4½ months of history, none of these is reliable yet.

What I keep in mind

  • It is early. 4½ months of NAVs is too short to judge any fund, and the annualised figure is only an extrapolation.
  • Credit risk is the story. About three-quarters of the debt is rated below AAA. Higher yield comes with a risk of default, so I look at the credit mix every month.
  • Size changes things. A fund that grows 18% in a month has to put new money to work. October went mostly to shares and cash.
  • Know the terms. Minimum investment ₹10 lakh. Exit load 2% if sold within 1 year.
  • Holdings change. Everything here is as of 30 Sep and 31 Oct. None of it is a recommendation.
