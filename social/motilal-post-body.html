At a glance

On 21 January 2026 the equity side of Motilal Oswal Midcap Fund passed from Niket Shah to Ajay Khandelwal, Varun Sharma and Ankit Agarwal. Since then the fund (Regular Plan, Growth) is up 9.1% to 20 November, against 6.5% for the Nifty Midcap 150 index fund. It got there the hard way: down 15.4% to 31 March, then up 29.0%. Over a full year it is still slightly behind (+0.3% against +1.2%). All figures are absolute, to 20 November 2026 NAVs.

What I keep in mind

  1. Ten months is short. A good run after a change of managers is encouraging, not proof.
  2. Midcaps swing. The same fund lost 15% in two months. Be ready for that before you hold it.
  3. The index line is a stand-in. It is an index fund's NAV, not the TRI, so small gaps can come from that.
  4. Portfolio weights are from two factsheets. They show the top 10 only, not every holding.
