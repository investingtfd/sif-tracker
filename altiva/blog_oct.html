Why a rising index did not lift the fund

According to the fund's October note, three things came together. Open each one.

1. The rally was narrow

The Nifty 50 rose 1.91%, led by IT and a few PSU stocks. A handful of names carrying an index up does not mean the broader market was rising with it.

2. The wider F&O universe fell

The universe of stocks with futures and options fell about 3% in the month, and its weaker half fell about 9%. That weighed on the fund's derivative strategies, which are built around that universe rather than around the index.

3. Yields rose

Higher bond yields marked down prices of the fund's bonds and REITs. This is a mark-to-market effect: it shows up in the daily NAV even if the securities are held to maturity.

What I keep in mind

A first year with a clear lead over the benchmark is encouraging, but I try not to read too much into it.

  • One year is a short record. The fund has seen one sharp market fall (March) and two soft months in a row. It has not yet been through a long, drawn-out one.
  • Derivative strategies can have bad months. October showed that when the wider F&O universe falls, the fund can lag even in a rising index.
  • The mix changes. The split above is a snapshot, and it moved between August and October.
  • It is a SIF, not a regular mutual fund. The ₹10 lakh minimum across Altiva strategies and the higher risk mean it is not for every portfolio.
